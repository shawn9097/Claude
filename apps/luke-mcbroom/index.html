— Honest answers

Asked & answered.

I'm too out of shape to start. Will you judge me?

No. Genuinely. Half my clients started exactly where you are — feeling out of shape, intimidated, sure they'd be the worst person in the gym. None of them were. We start where you are, build slowly, and let the wins compound. Kindness is in my actual brand for a reason.

I don't have a gym membership. Can you still help?

Yes. The 8-Week At-Home program needs zero equipment. The At-Home Free Weight program needs a couple of dumbbells. Full online coaching gets a custom plan built around exactly what you have access to — at home, at a gym, on the road.

I've tried trainers before and it didn't stick. Why would this be different?

Most programs fail because they're built for someone who isn't you. We build yours around your life — your schedule, your kitchen, your stress, your sleep. Then I check in weekly and adjust. The accountability isn't pressure. It's a partner.

What if I'm not local to Cookeville?

Online coaching works everywhere there's WiFi. You'll get the same custom training, nutrition coaching, weekly check-ins, and direct messaging — just delivered through the app instead of in the gym.

How much does full online coaching cost?

It's application-based and priced based on the level of support you need. The free 15-min call is where we figure out the right fit — and if it's not a fit, I'll tell you that too.

How fast will I see results?

Most clients feel stronger within 2 weeks, see real changes in the mirror by 6-8 weeks, and have meaningful body composition changes by 12 weeks. The catch: that timeline assumes you actually do the work. I'll help you do it.

Ready to start?

Free 15-min call. No pressure. No pitch. We'll just see if we click — and you'll leave with a clearer picture of what to do next, even if we don't end up working together.
