# Underdog City — Brand & Identity

Living doc. The settled answers about what Underdog City *is* and who its face is.

## What it is
A **transmedia franchise** — a dark-fantasy world where music is the front door, with the serial/story, community (Tenants), and eventually anime to follow. Not "just a band."

## Artist name
Released as **Underdog City** on streaming / DistroKid.

## The Face: Kazo & the Crowned King (the man vs. the myth)
One soul, two distances:

- **Kazo — the man.** The actual character. **Crownless.** Doesn't want a crown, doesn't chase rule. Grounded, human — the person behind the music. (This is the figure in the photorealistic "Music/Band" reference art.)
- **The Crowned King — the myth.** *Not literally Kazo* — it's how Underdog City mythologizes him: "the King of the Bottom," ruler of the undercity, a legend the people project onto him. Charcoal mask, gold kintsugi cracks, molten-gold eyes, broken gold crown. The iconic, symbolic face (album cover, site, logo-level brand image).

**Why it works:** it *is* the story. The magic system's highest state is **the Crown** — accept the fracture, rule from the bottom. A man who rejects the crown yet becomes mythologized as the crowned king mirrors the whole thesis: *"You call it rock bottom. I call it a throne."* It also reflects how fans always build a legend bigger than the person.

**Branding implication:** the **myth (masked crowned king)** = the hero icon / logo / symbol. **Kazo the man** = the human underdog story underneath it. Two representations, one soul — complementary, not competing.

## Voice — "The King is seen; the man is heard"
- **Fan-facing (captions, comment replies, DMs): Kazo the man.** Raw, human, real-talk. Matches Shawn's own voice (casual, warm, real, allergic to AI-sounding polish) and is the sustainable choice — just be yourself. This is what builds belonging; misfits bond with the human, not the throne.
- **In-world / hype (video on-screen text, lore drops, proclamations): the Crowned King.** Mythic, defiant, in-character — the legend register.
- Rule of thumb: the **icon is mythic; the person who talks to you is real.** *(Confirmed.)*

## AI / Suno origin stance — DECIDED
**Own it quietly, in the background.** Never hidden, never the headline. Woven into the underdog myth and used to *empower*, not explain:
> "One man at rock bottom built an entire universe for misfits using tools everyone underestimated."

Framing: the tools were underestimated — *just like the misfits are.* AI is another underdog in the story, not a dirty secret. Don't lead with "this is AI"; let people find the human story first and let the tool be part of the triumph, never the pitch.
