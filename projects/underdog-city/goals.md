# Underdog City — Wants & Goals

The aiming point for the whole project. When a decision is unclear, check it against this.

## The Why (the soul)
Underdog City exists to be a home for **the underdogs, the misfits, the weirdos** — somewhere to belong. That mission inspired everything: the music, the story, and the magic system all grew from it. People don't rally around an album; they rally around belonging. This is the realest asset.

## North Star
Grow Underdog City into a world big and alive enough to be **picked up as an anime** — and earn a **living** from it along the way. This is the all-in, main bet.

## The Shape of It
- **A transmedia world, not just an album.** Pillars: Music, the Serial (story/lore), Community (Tenants), and eventually Visuals/Animation.
- **The album (THRONE AT THE BOTTOM, 07.31.2026) is chapter one** — the front door to the world, not the destination.
- The path to an anime runs through **proof the world has an audience and enough story to adapt.** The anime goal and the audience/income goals are the same road, not competing ones.

## Who It's For
The core is a **psychographic, not a demographic**: misfits and outsiders looking for somewhere to belong. They overlap across three fan pools:
- Metal / heavy-music fans (nu-metal, metalcore, rap-metal)
- Anime & manga fans (dark-fantasy worlds)
- Story & lore fans (deep worldbuilding, serialized fiction)

The exact core is still crystallizing — sharpening it is an early job.

## Near-Term Goal (next 6-12 months)
**Start earning** — but via the honest order below, because income follows attention.

### The sequence: Attention → Audience → Income
1. **Attention** — content that makes people *find* the world (the current gap).
2. **Audience** — turn discovery into Tenants who actually belong and stick.
3. **Income** — monetize that audience (streams, merch, community, etc.) once it exists.

## Current Focus
Not making *more* music or story — there's enough. The active work is **content for discovery**: short-form and social content that pulls new people to Underdog City.

## Biggest Constraint
**Knowing what to do next** (decision paralysis / overwhelm), not money or material. So we work **one clear next-action at a time** and keep a single home base so nothing lives only in Shawn's head.

## Success Markers (rough, to refine)
- Near-term: a growing, engaged Tenant base and first real income.
- Long-term: a world with enough audience + story that an anime adaptation becomes a conversation people take seriously.
