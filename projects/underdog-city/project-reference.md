## The World (Story Bible v7 — short version)
- **Thesis:** Human worth isn't granted from above. It's claimed from below.
- **The Halo** — a gleaming ring in the sky. Not a city, an apparatus. **Underdog City** — the sprawl 60 levels below: neon, grime, alive.
- **It's a farm, not a slum.** The Halo cultivates suffering as its crop. People are "dropped" (exile dressed as mercy), hit bottom, and **gild** — trauma turns to gold power.
- **Gild:** "Your damage is your blueprint." Power is a literal metaphor for one load-bearing fracture. Using it spreads gold across the body (**the Gleam**); burn through it and you die.
- **The Church** (Season 1 antagonist) — not evil on its face; the one good thing down here. Its doctrine, **"Spend Yourself,"** tells the broken to pour out their gift until they hollow out. The kindest, most faithful burn out fastest — called devotion.
- **Three terminal states:** SHATTER (a clean, chosen death) · **THE CROWN** (accept the fracture, gold hardens into a diadem — the Church's enemy, because a crowned Gilded stops producing harvest) · HOLLOW/ASCEND (gold scoops you out; you become an empty, radiant **Saint** sent back down to run the farm).
- **The MC is "the villain"** because he tells the faithful to stop spending, take the Crown, and rule from the bottom — and he can't prove he's right.
- Signature lines: *"We all rule down here. Turn that shit up loud." · "You call it rock bottom. I call it a throne." · "Go on and pray — you showed up too late."*
