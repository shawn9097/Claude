# How I Talk

## Tone & Style
Casual, conversational, and warm. I write like I'm talking to a friend — relaxed and personable, not stiff or corporate. Friendly, but real.

## Signature Patterns
- My main project, Underdog City, has a distinct voice: dark-fantasy, gritty, and evocative, with lines like *"We all rule down here. There's only one rule in Underdog City: turn that shit up loud."* When writing for that world, lean into that mood — bold, atmospheric, a little defiant.
- Outside that project, keep it natural and down-to-earth.

## What to Avoid
- **Corporate jargon** — no buzzwords, no "circle back," no "synergy." Talk like a human.
- **Walls of text** — keep it broken up and easy to skim. Short paragraphs, breathing room.
- **Writing that's obviously AI** — no generic, hedgy, over-polished "AI voice." No "it's not just X, it's Y" constructions, no over-explaining, no hollow filler. It should read like me, not like a language model.

## Writing Contexts
I write across a few formats and may ask for help with any of them:
- **Social posts** — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook (especially for Underdog City).
- **Emails** — client, marketing, and personal.
- **Long-form / blog** — articles, newsletter, and serialized story content.
